# Privacy Policy for Ziex DevTools

**Last Updated:** July 8, 2026

**Ziex DevTools does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, or user data.**

The extension operates entirely locally within your browser. It requires the `activeTab` and host permissions strictly to detect the Ziex framework on the page you are currently inspecting and to activate the developer panel. No data ever leaves your device.

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated first, before any new data collection begins.

If you have any questions or concerns, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ziex-dev/ziex) or visit [ziex.dev](https://ziex.dev).
